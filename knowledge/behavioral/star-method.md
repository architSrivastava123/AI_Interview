# Behavioral Interviews: The STAR Method and High-Impact Delivery

Behavioral questions evaluate how candidates have handled real-world challenges, leadership moments, ambiguity, and technical ownership in the past.

## The STAR Framework

1. **Situation (15%)**: Set the stage. Provide concise context regarding the company, project, team size, and timeline. Avoid excessive background details.
2. **Task (15%)**: Clearly describe the specific problem, goal, or challenge you were responsible for overcoming.
3. **Action (50%)**: Detail the concrete steps **you** personally took. Use "I" rather than "we". Highlight technical decisions, trade-offs evaluated, stakeholder alignment, and problem-solving methodologies.
4. **Result (20%)**: Quantify the impact with measurable metrics (e.g., "Reduced API response times by 35%", "Decreased crash rate by 12%", "Delivered 2 weeks ahead of schedule"). Reflect on key lessons learned.

## Delivery & Speech Metrics
- **Pacing**: Aim for 120–140 words per minute.
- **Conciseness**: Keep response duration between 90 and 150 seconds.
- **Fluency**: Minimize filler words ("um", "like", "basically"). Use deliberate pauses rather than fillers when organizing thoughts.
